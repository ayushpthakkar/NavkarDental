Our story

A dedicated orthodontic & dental practice in Thaltej.

Navkar Orthodontic & Dental Clinic was founded to bring specialist orthodontic expertise together with complete family dental care, all under one roof at Maple Trade Center, Thaltej. We focus on personalised treatment plans, modern equipment, and a calm, welcoming environment for every patient who walks in.

Led by Dr. Aesha Acharya Thakkar, our team combines clinical precision with clear communication — so you always understand what's happening and why, before any treatment begins.

  • ✓ Patient-centred, customised treatment plans
  • ✓ Modern, hygienically maintained facilities
  • ✓ Punctual, appointment-based scheduling
  • ✓ Comprehensive care from routine checkups to advanced orthodontics
Lead Doctor

Dr. Aesha Acharya Thakkar

B.D.S., M.D.S. — Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopedics

Dr. Aesha specialises in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics, holding a B.D.S. and M.D.S. in the field. She is a certified Invisalign practitioner and serves as a Senior Lecturer at the College of Dental Sciences and Research Center, Ahmedabad — combining an academic's precision with a practising clinician's hands-on care.
